Calling and spiritual formation

Neshamah is the Hebrew word for breath. In Genesis 2:7 God breathes life into the first human. Everything in this model draws on that breath: a leader's calling, prayer, Scripture and character come first, and skills are built on top.

  • A daily rhythm of prayer and Scripture
  • Character reviewed with a mentor
  • A written statement of your calling that you revisit every 90 days

What the model is built to serve

Every part of the pathway answers to our vision and mission. If a piece of training does not serve them, it does not belong in the model.

Our vision

Leaders in the church and in society who lead from a life filled with God, and who finish the work they are called to.

Our mission

To form leaders through teaching, practice, example and community, anchored in prayer, Scripture and character, with a clear plan for every season of their calling.

It begins with breath

Skill without formation produces leaders who can start things but cannot sustain them. So the foundation of the pathway is not a course. It is the leader's life with God and the calling they carry.

Neshamah: the breath of life God breathed into humanity (Genesis 2:7).

  • Prayer and Scripture are part of the weekly rhythm, not an optional extra.
  • Character is reviewed with a mentor, because the work outgrows the leader who has not been shaped for it.
  • Each learner writes a statement of their calling and returns to it at every 90-day reset.

Four ways leaders learn

People do not grow through classrooms alone. The pathway uses four methods together, and every learner's plan draws on all four.

Education

Learn what leaders need to know

Structured teaching, reading and Scripture study give you language and frameworks for leadership, communication and self-awareness. This is where you learn what to do and why.

How it works

  • Lessons are released in batches so the cohort moves together.
  • Reading and written reflection turn information into understanding.
  • Your scores and attendance are recorded on your student record.

Experience

Practice in real settings

Leadership is learned by leading. Each 90-day plan includes a stretch assignment in your own ministry or business, with real people and real stakes.

How it works

  • One stretch assignment per cycle, chosen with your mentor.
  • A team, a project or a budget you are responsible for.
  • A written note on what worked, what failed and what you learned.

Exposure

Watch leaders lead

Some things cannot be taught in a lesson: how a leader decides under pressure, handles conflict and treats people. We place you in front of leaders who are further along so you can see it.

How it works

  • Guest leaders from ministry and the marketplace.
  • Visits to churches and organisations.
  • A debrief after each: what did I see, and what will I change?

Community

You do not lead alone

Learning sticks when it is shared. Your cohort, your mentor and your accountability partners keep you honest, encouraged and finishing. In Nehemiah 3 the wall rose because families built their sections side by side.

How it works

  • A cohort that moves through the pathway together.
  • A mentor who reviews your plan and your progress.
  • Regular check-ins on goals and on character.

Three seasons of a leader's life

A leader's needs change with the season. The pathway plans for where you are, where you are heading and what is about to change.

Immediate

Lead well where you are now

We start with your current role and ask what you need in the next 90 days to lead it with more skill and less strain. One focus area, one stretch assignment, one weekly rhythm.

Intermediate

Prepare for the next role

Most leaders are already growing into something. We name that next role and build the skills, relationships and exposure it will require before you step into it.

Transitional

Steward a change of season

Planting a church, handing over leadership, moving between ministry and the marketplace, leading through loss. Transitions break leaders who have not prepared for them, so we plan for them early.

A 90-day loop that keeps the model alive

A model that does not repeat is only a diagram. Every learner works in 90-day cycles, and each cycle ends by feeding the next.

  1. Survey

    Assessments such as DISC, emotional intelligence and the Johari Window, plus a conversation about your calling. Set goals for the cycle.

  2. Equip

    Lessons, reading and time with leaders who are further along.

  3. Build

    Apply what you are learning in a stretch assignment.

  4. Review

    Reflect with your cohort and mentor on what happened and why.

  5. Renew

    Reassess at day 90, celebrate progress and reset the plan.

At day 90 your new assessment results are compared with the first, so growth is something you can see, not only something you feel.
