About Neshamah

Neshamah Leadership Institute serves primarily the body of God in leadership development, both the pulpit ministers and the marketplace apostles, for effectiveness and dominion.

What you will gain

  1. Clarity about your calling. A sharper understanding of your gifts, strengths and assignment.
  2. Self-awareness. Honest insight into your leadership style, emotional intelligence and blind spots, through our leadership assessments.
  3. Stronger character. The inner life and integrity that sustain influence over time.
  4. Practical leadership skills. How to communicate, make decisions, build teams and turn vision into action.
  5. Influence in your sphere. Whether you lead a congregation or a company, you learn to carry Kingdom values into it.
  6. A community of leaders. Relationships, accountability and a network of pulpit and marketplace leaders who sharpen each other.

Our vision

To see leaders across the body of Christ, in the pulpit and in the marketplace, who lead with spiritual depth, sound character and proven effectiveness, and who exercise godly dominion in every sphere they are called to.

Our mission

Neshamah Leadership Institute equips pulpit ministers and marketplace apostles through biblical teaching, practical leadership training and honest self-discovery, so that they lead with greater effectiveness, build lasting works and carry the Kingdom’s influence into their churches, businesses and communities.

Our core values

  • Word-rooted: Scripture is the foundation of everything we teach.
  • Character before charisma: who you are matters more than how you appear.
  • Excellence: we serve God with our best preparation, thinking and effort.
  • Integrity: private conduct and public leadership must match.
  • Stewardship: gifts, time, money and influence are held in trust for God.
  • Unity and honour: leaders from every setting learn to value and serve one another.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Neshamah Leadership Institute for?

It serves the body of Christ, especially pulpit ministers and marketplace apostles: pastors, ministry leaders, business owners, professionals and anyone carrying leadership responsibility who wants to be more effective.

What difference will this make to me?

You should finish with clearer direction, stronger leadership habits and better self-understanding. We aim to equip you to lead more effectively, but the results depend on how fully you apply what you learn.

I am a pulpit minister. How will this help my ministry?

You’ll strengthen how you lead people, manage teams, handle conflict and plan for growth, so that your preaching and pastoring are supported by sound leadership.

I work in the marketplace. Is this relevant to me?

Yes. Your workplace is your assignment. The training helps you lead with integrity and vision in business and professional life, and to see your work as ministry.

What do you mean by “dominion”?

Dominion means godly stewardship and influence: leading well in the area God has given you, so that people, organisations and communities are better for it. It does not mean control or power for its own sake.

How will I know I am improving?

You’ll complete leadership assessments such as DISC, Emotional Intelligence, Strengths Compass and the Johari Window, and receive a personal report. You’ll also take lesson quizzes and receive transcripts with your results and attendance.

Will it change my character, or only my skills?

Both. Skills without character don’t last, so we pair practical training with spiritual formation, honest feedback and accountability.
